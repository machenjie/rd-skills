# Review Rubric

## Passing Standard

Both functions satisfy the executable record contract. Assertions must distinguish valid evidence from unsupported conclusions.

## Scoring

- 50 percent causal and experiment decision correctness.
- 30 percent independent arithmetic, set, and ordering assertions.
- 20 percent input preservation and explicit proof limits.

## Automatic Failure Conditions

- Promoting a difference or cleanup command to verified cause.
- Accepting a runnable mock demo as critical boundary evidence.
- Ignoring early stopping, budget exhaustion, or incomparable inputs.
- Calling a prototype production-ready.
- Accepting the delta-is-cause faulty control.

## Reviewer Notes

Inspect observed facts and intervention outcomes. Fixture acceptance does not prove the observations occurred on a real host.
