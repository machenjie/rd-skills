# Review Rubric

## Passing Standard

Calculate exact timing and fixed steps. Reject incomplete playback and stale device evidence. Preserve the separation between submission, completion, visibility, and artifact identity.

## Scoring

Assess independently derived times, conservation, event order, identity, policy boundaries, and honest recorded-evidence limits.

## Automatic Failure Conditions

Approving upload or connection as playback, counting paused time as simulation time, reading after submission alone, or accepting stale shader/layout metadata fails.

## Reviewer Notes

No real media, GPU, game, driver, or device executes. The fixture does not establish WebGPU, Vulkan, Metal, CUDA, or HIP equivalence.
