# Failure Contract Design Evidence Patterns

- Treat repository inspection, generated contracts, and prior task evidence as leads; current negative paths and boundary tests determine verified claims.

## Claim Evidence

- **Taxonomy:** state table, typed code/result, owner boundary, and negative assertion; does not cover every provider shape.
- **Translation:** raw failure, local type, public mapping, authorized diagnostic mapping, cause, correlation, and redaction; does not prove unknown wrappers or sinks.
- **Retry meaning:** timeout/cancellation/unknown stance, caller decision, specialist route, and negative path; does not prove keys, timing, deduplication, queue behavior, or recovery.
- **Partial/degraded:** completed/incomplete effects, reduced-quality meaning, external representation, residual owner, and specialist route; does not prove compensation, fallback, or reconciliation success.
- **Freshness:** rerun after taxonomy, translation, representation, fallback, partial/degraded, generated-contract, test, or validation changes.

State command/result, final edit scope, untested chaos/live-provider/production paths, unavailable generated clients, skipped recovery, proof limits, residual owner, and protected-data leakage checks.

## Anti-Patterns

- Reject null/generic success, collapsed categories, raw dependency errors, unsafe retryability, hidden partials, and cancellation-as-failure.
