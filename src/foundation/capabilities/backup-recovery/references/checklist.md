# Backup Recovery Checklist

- Name the failure scenario, recovery unit, authoritative and derived state, dependency graph, and accountable objective source.
- Derive tolerated loss and outage from consequence, scope, scale, and dependencies, with the achieved exercise result and gap recorded as evidence.
- Identify backup or log artifact, capture point, lineage, key/schema/application compatibility, restore target, and access authority.
- Define capture consistency, quiesce or checkpoint needs, restore order, replay, duplicate/missing side effects, and reconciliation.
- Select isolation, immutability, encryption, deletion protection, and credential separation from the named failure model.
- Validate domain invariants, counts/checks where meaningful, key access, and representative business read/write or reconciliation behavior.
- Cover retention, key history, legal hold, erasure, expiry, and late replay without resurrecting deleted or unauthorized state.
- Record environment, scale, region, dependency, provider, wall-clock, and live-authority limits plus the residual owner.
- Name the material changes that make current recovery evidence stale and require re-exercise.
