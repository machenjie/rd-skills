# Business Invariant Checklist

- Define the exact business term, owner, object, actor, and source of authority.
- List allowed, denied, boundary, retry, and duplicate scenarios.
- For the changed invariant, trace reachable mutation and decision entry points in the accepted scope, including jobs and support tools; record inaccessible or dynamic paths as evidence limits.
- Choose one authoritative enforcement location and identify bypasses.
- Map each rule and forbidden transition to executable proof or an explicit gap.
- Stop for an owner decision when money, entitlement, permission, compliance,
  audit, irreversible data, or historical interpretation is ambiguous.
