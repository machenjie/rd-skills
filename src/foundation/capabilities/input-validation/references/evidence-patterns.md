# Input Validation Evidence Patterns

Load this reference when authority, representation, canonicalization, bounds, state, replay, write-surface, rejection, compatibility, or external-response claims require fresh proof. Do not use it as a generic validation tutorial.

| Claim | Minimum fresh evidence | Proof limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source and authority are known | Current entry point, actor or system, caller-controlled fields, authoritative writer, parser/enforcement owner, downstream consumer, and alternate-path classification | Does not establish unknown callers or adjacent authentication and permission policy |
| Representation order is unambiguous | Raw/decoded/parsed/normalized/canonical sequence, parser configuration, ambiguity/duplicate behavior, decision representation, and bypass case | Does not prove untested library, encoding, locale, or protocol behavior |
| Shape and resource bounds protect the consumer | Consumer contract, selected constraints, unknown-field behavior, nesting/collection/byte/parser-work case, amplification trace, and compatibility fixture | Does not establish production payload diversity or deployment capacity |
| Current-state validation reaches the effect | Invariant authority, state/version lookup, revalidation or guard near effect, replay/duplicate indicators checked against owning command semantics, stale/replay/duplicate case, conflict outcome, and side-effect trace | Does not prove business uniqueness, idempotency key/result reuse, retry policy, duplicate side-effect outcome, untested interleavings, or external state |
| Writable fields are bounded | Explicit command or mapper, accepted field set, sensitive-field classification, raw-binding scan, new-field case, and persistence/effect trace | Does not establish permission correctness or future model changes |
| Rejection and compatibility are owned | Failure classification, safe location/remediation, disclosure policy, response and redacted diagnostic sample, affected consumer, and old-valid case | Does not prove localization, SDK, support, log, or deployed-client behavior outside inspected cases |
| External responses are validated before use | Current adapter/client/parser, expected authority/version/shape/semantics/freshness/bounds, malformed or stale response case, mapping/effect trace, and failure outcome | Does not prove provider production behavior or unrepresented response variants |

## Freshness And Closure

- Treat prior schemas, generated clients, provider docs, incident notes, validation reports, samples, and compaction summaries as search leads until current entry points, parsers, consumers, state, mappings, and owner match.
- Re-run applicable negative and compatibility cases after the final route, schema, parser, normalization, mapper, state check, provider client, fixture, generated artifact, or rejection-contract edit.
- Map final confidence to current source/config paths, parsed outcomes, fixtures, consumer evidence, owner evidence, and explicit residual entry points, payloads, states, and external behavior.
- Keep production payload diversity, provider behavior, deployment limits, future fields, unknown callers, untested interleavings, business idempotency outcomes, and adjacent web/authentication/permission/threat decisions outside the proven boundary unless independently verified.
