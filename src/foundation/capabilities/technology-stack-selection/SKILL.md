# technology-stack-selection

## Registry Trigger

**Use when**

- A framework, platform, datastore, infrastructure, or managed-service commitment is open.

**Do not use when**

- Language, package, build, or configuration choice is primary.
- Policy fixes the stack and no commitment or migration changes.

## Skill Role

Own stack compatibility, operation, lifecycle, cost, migration, and exit.

## High-Value Rules

- Screen hard constraints before comparing preferences.
- Bind the commitment to current ownership, lifecycle, transition, exit, and proof evidence.
- Load the benchmark when a material stack choice remains open.

## Anti-Patterns

- Local success is not stack-selection evidence.

## Stop Conditions

- Return adjacent mechanics to their owners.

## Output Contract

- technology-stack decision with hard-constraint fit existing-stack comparison ownership lifecycle supply chain total-change cost migration coexistence exit proof limits and residual risk
