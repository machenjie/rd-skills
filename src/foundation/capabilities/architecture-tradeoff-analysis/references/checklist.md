# Architecture Decision Closure Checklist

- State the decision question, owner, affected boundary, in-scope outcome, and excluded decisions.
- Source hard constraints and define the evidence that establishes or rejects feasibility.
- Classify candidate options as feasible, disqualified, or unresolved before comparing preferences.
- Exclude disqualified and unresolved candidates before comparing preferences; keep uncertainty and evidence strength visible.
- Record material consequences for changed ownership, behavior, trust/data boundaries, migration, operations, cost, delivery, validation, or future change.
- Define the selected option's reversibility, exit work, retained obligations, authority, and validation path.
- Map each decisive claim and material residual risk to a falsifying check, fresh artifact, owner, or explicit proof limit.
- Define assumption-change triggers, owned signal sources, and the decision consequence when triggered.
- Record unresolved feasibility, skipped boundaries, unverified consumers, and unavailable evidence as blockers or residual risk according to impact and exit difficulty.
