# Evidence Reuse And Contradiction Patterns

Use these patterns after the claim and scope have been fixed.

| Pattern | Decision | Required record |
|---|---|---|
| Same claim, scope, input identity, and mechanism | Reusable when no relevant invalidating change is present | Artifact identity, freshness basis, and reuse decision |
| Source or provenance is missing | Invalid because the artifact cannot be bound to its producer or inputs | Missing provenance and affected claim |
| Scope or input identity differs | Invalid for the current claim even if the result is recent | Exact mismatch and uncovered scope |
| Procedure, environment, or observable result is unavailable | Insufficient for reproducibility | Missing fact and resulting proof limit |
| Two artifacts support incompatible results | Contradictory until their scope, inputs, or producers explain the difference | Both artifact identities and unresolved relationship |

A classification states what the artifact proves, its proof limit, and the residual uncertainty. A reusable artifact remains bounded to the claim and scope recorded in its assessment.
