# Downstream Transfer Selection Checklist

Use this checklist after work for one downstream consumer and purpose.

- State the downstream consumer and purpose, plus the decision or next action they own.
- Bind every decision-changing claim to its exact artifact, source identity, and latest diff when code changed.
- Attach fresh validation only to supported claims; record coverage and proof limits.
- Preserve unresolved decisions, constraints, findings, owner, and next action.
- Record exclusions and omissions with their possible downstream impact.
- Record staleness and reload triggers for material artifact, diff, validation, constraint, or finding changes.
- Preserve contradictory evidence and the fact needed to reconcile it.
- Flag lossy transfer when compression removes identity, qualification, uncertainty, or a proof limit.
- Return residual uncertainty that remains after selection.
