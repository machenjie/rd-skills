# Business Invariant Evidence

Use this reference only when rule ownership, lifecycle, entitlement, money,
permission, or tenant boundaries materially affect the task.

- Define the business vocabulary and authoritative object owner.
- Trace each decision and mutation entry point to its enforcement location.
- List allowed, denied, boundary, duplicate, retry, and historical-data cases.
- Separate current source fact, inference, assumption, and open owner question.
- Map each invariant and forbidden transition to executable proof or a named gap.
- Stop for an owner decision when ambiguity affects money, entitlement,
  permission, compliance, audit, irreversible data, or historical meaning.
